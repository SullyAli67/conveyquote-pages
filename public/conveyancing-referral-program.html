The programme

A revenue line that fits the business you already run

Every client you help to move home needs conveyancing. Most agents and brokers hand that moment away — to a comparison site, a lender's panel, or whichever firm the client stumbles across — and earn nothing from it. Worse, they lose visibility of the one workstream most likely to delay or collapse the sale they worked for.

The ConveyQuote referral programme turns that moment into a proper revenue line. You refer the client through your partner portal in about two minutes. We prepare a fixed, itemised quote, reviewed by a real person before it is sent, and place the matter with an SRA-regulated solicitor from our panel. You track every case to completion and earn a disclosed fee on each one that completes.

No joining cost, no minimum volume, no targets. Just a clean, compliant way to earn from work that is already passing through your hands — with the referral fee disclosed to your client exactly as the SRA's transparency rules require.

How it works

Three steps from introduction to income

1

You meet the client

A buyer, seller, remortgager or landlord crosses your desk — exactly as they do today. Nothing about your sales process changes.

2

You refer in about two minutes

Enter the key details in your partner portal and see an itemised quote preview instantly. Add a personal note, and the quote goes to your client with your name attached — reviewed by our team before it is issued.

3

You earn on completion

The matter is handled by an SRA-regulated panel solicitor while you watch progress in your portal. When the transaction completes, your fee is recorded against the case — typically £100–£300 per completed transaction, depending on transaction type — agreed with you up front, fixed per case type, no clawback surprises.

Commission structure

Clear rates, agreed up front, nothing hidden

Commission is typically £100–£300 per completed transaction, depending on transaction type — agreed with you up front, fixed per case type, no clawback surprises. Your rates are confirmed in writing when you join, and they do not move mid-relationship.

Principle What it means in practice
Fixed per case type Each transaction type — purchase, sale, sale and purchase, remortgage, transfer of equity — carries an agreed fee. You always know what a referral is worth before you make it.
Paid on completion Commission is linked to completed transactions, tracked case by case in your portal. Because nothing is paid out on quotes alone, there are no clawbacks when a client changes their mind.
Never funded by your client The referral fee is paid by the panel firm and disclosed to your client, as the SRA's transparency rules require. Your client's quote is not inflated to pay you.
No costs to you No joining fee, no subscription, no charge for quotes or for referrals that do not proceed. Money flows one way: to you.

Security & compliance

Built on regulation, not workarounds

Every solicitor on our panel is SRA-regulated. The legal work on every matter you refer is carried out by an independent firm regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, with the professional obligations, supervision and insurance that brings. Referral fees are disclosed to your client exactly as the SRA's transparency rules require — done properly, disclosure builds trust rather than denting it.

Your client's data is handled under UK GDPR. Details travel over encrypted connections and sit on encrypted infrastructure, and they are used for one purpose: progressing the matter you referred. ConveyQuote is operated by Essentially Law Limited, registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (registration CSN9542473).

Your client remains your client. We do not market to the people you refer, we do not sell or share their data beyond the solicitor handling their matter, and we do not cross-sell other services to them. We exist to handle the legal-side plumbing — the relationship you have built stays yours.

Common questions

Quick answers for new partners

Do my clients know about ConveyQuote?

Yes — your client's quote arrives from ConveyQuote with your name and personal note attached, and the referral fee is disclosed exactly as the SRA's rules require. Done openly, the introduction strengthens your relationship. We never market to the people you refer.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. There is no joining fee, no subscription, and no charge for quotes or for referrals that do not proceed.

When do I earn?

Commission is recorded against each matter when the transaction completes, and your portal's payments view shows what you have earned at any moment. Because nothing is paid on quotes alone, there are no clawbacks.

Is there a minimum volume?

No. One referral a quarter or fifty a month — the same agreed rates apply from your first case.
