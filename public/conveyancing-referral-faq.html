Common questions

Everything partners ask before joining

If your question is not covered below, email info@conveyquote.uk — a person, not a queue, will reply.

Do my clients know about ConveyQuote?

Yes — and that is deliberate. Your client's quote arrives from ConveyQuote with your name and your personal note attached, so they know exactly who introduced them and why. The SRA's transparency rules require referral fees to be disclosed, and we handle that disclosure properly on every matter. Done openly, the introduction strengthens your relationship rather than undermining it. We never contact your client about anything beyond the matter you referred.

How quickly do quotes come back?

You see an itemised quote preview in the portal the moment you submit a referral — before anything is sent to your client. The quote itself is then reviewed by a real person and issued by email within one working day (Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm). Most arrive considerably faster. Because the review happens before issue, the figure your client receives is one they can actually rely on.

What if a quote is rejected or the client doesn't proceed?

Nothing happens — and nothing is owed by anyone. Quotes are free, carry no obligation, and a client who is not ready today can come back when they are. You can also re-quote the same client later from your portal if their circumstances change, for example when a purchase falls through and they find another property. Commission is only ever linked to completed transactions, so there are no clawbacks to worry about.

Can I refer remortgages and additional purchases?

Yes. The portal handles purchases, sales, combined sale-and-purchase, remortgages and transfers of equity. Remortgages deserve particular attention: a client you refer for a purchase this year is a potential remortgage referral in two to five years, which turns a one-off introduction into a recurring revenue line. Buy-to-let investors and multi-property households work the same way.

How do I track commissions and payments?

Your portal dashboard shows referrals sent this month, active matters, completed matters and fees earned, all in one view. Each referral shows its live case status — from client care sent through searches, exchange and completion — plus an estimated completion date where the solicitor has set one. The payments view lists what has been earned matter by matter, so reconciliation takes minutes rather than email chains.

What happens if something goes wrong?

The legal work always sits with an SRA-regulated firm, which means a formal complaints procedure, professional indemnity insurance and the Legal Ombudsman as a backstop — protections an unregulated arrangement cannot offer. Alongside that, we track every case status centrally and you have a direct line to our team, not a call centre. If a matter stalls, you will see it in the portal and we will already be chasing it.

Is there a minimum volume requirement?

No. Some partners refer one client a quarter; others refer dozens a month. The commission rates agreed with you apply from your first referral, and there is no joining fee, no monthly fee and no target to hit before you earn. If your volume grows, talk to us — partnership terms can grow with it.

Can I integrate with my agency software?

Referrals are portal-based today, and deliberately quick: around two minutes per referral with an instant quote preview. There is no public API yet, and we would rather say so than overpromise. If you run meaningful volume or a multi-branch operation, integration and consolidated reporting are exactly the conversation to have as part of a broker partnership — email info@conveyquote.uk and we will scope it with you honestly.

What support do you provide?

You get direct access to the team by email (info@conveyquote.uk) and phone (07592 654 666) — the people who actually review the quotes, not a script-driven helpline. New partners get a walkthrough of the portal, and we provide plain-English guidance your negotiators can use when introducing the service to clients. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. There is no joining fee, no subscription, and no charge for quotes, declined referrals or clients who do not proceed. Commission flows to you, not from you. Just as importantly, your client's quote is not inflated to fund your commission — the referral fee is paid by the panel firm and disclosed to the client, exactly as the SRA's transparency rules require.
