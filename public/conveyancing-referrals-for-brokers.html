Scale

Scale your conveyancing revenue across the whole operation

A single agent referring conveyancing earns a useful side income. A brokerage or network doing it consistently across every negotiator and every branch builds a genuine revenue line. The mechanics are the same two-minute referral — the difference is coordination: shared standards for when and how clients are introduced, visibility across the whole book, and commercial terms that reflect your volume.

That is what a ConveyQuote broker partnership is for. Each negotiator gets portal access and refers in the normal way; you get the consolidated picture — referrals, active matters, completions and fees earned — and a single commercial relationship covering the whole operation. Mortgage brokers fit the same model with a different rhythm: purchases at the front, then a long, dependable tail of remortgage referrals every time a fixed rate ends.

The numbers

A worked example — not a testimonial

The figures below are illustrative arithmetic using our standard commission range, not a claimed client result. They exist so you can sanity-check the opportunity against your own volumes.

A 20-agent network Illustration
Steady state 25 completed referrals a month across the network at an average £150 per case = £3,750/month, roughly £45,000 a year — from transactions the network was already handling.
Engaged state If each agent averages just 2 completed referrals a month (40 total) at £200 average, the line becomes £8,000/month, roughly £96,000 a year.
The remortgage tail Every purchase referred this year seeds a likely remortgage referral within two to five years — income that compounds without new transactions.

Run your own numbers: completed transactions per month × your agreed rate. If the result matters to your P&L, the conversation is worth having.

Security & compliance

Built on regulation, not workarounds

Every solicitor on our panel is SRA-regulated. The legal work on every matter you refer is carried out by an independent firm regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, with the professional obligations, supervision and insurance that brings. Referral fees are disclosed to your client exactly as the SRA's transparency rules require — done properly, disclosure builds trust rather than denting it.

Your client's data is handled under UK GDPR. Details travel over encrypted connections and sit on encrypted infrastructure, and they are used for one purpose: progressing the matter you referred. ConveyQuote is operated by Essentially Law Limited, registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (registration CSN9542473).

Your client remains your client. We do not market to the people you refer, we do not sell or share their data beyond the solicitor handling their matter, and we do not cross-sell other services to them. We exist to handle the legal-side plumbing — the relationship you have built stays yours.
