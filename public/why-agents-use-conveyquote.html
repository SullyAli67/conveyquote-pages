The trust factor

Your name goes on the introduction — it should be safe there

When you recommend conveyancing, you spend reputation. That is why trust comes first in our model: every matter is handled by an SRA-regulated panel firm, every quote is reviewed by a real person before issue so the figure holds, and pricing is fully itemised — legal fees, VAT, disbursements and a Stamp Duty estimate on separate lines. Referral fee disclosure is handled properly on every matter, exactly as the SRA's transparency rules require, so the arrangement is as clean on paper as it feels in practice.

The result: the client experience your name is attached to is fast, clear and regulated — and you can check on any matter's progress yourself rather than hoping.

The revenue factor

Real money from transactions you already handle

Commission is typically £100–£300 per completed transaction, depending on transaction type — agreed with you up front, fixed per case type, no clawback surprises. For an agency completing 100 referred transactions a year, that is roughly £15,000–£30,000 of annual revenue — no extra headcount, no regulatory burden, no office space. Sale-and-purchase households compound it (two matters from one client), and every purchase you refer seeds a likely remortgage referral within two to five years. The arithmetic is laid out fully in our revenue guide for agents.
