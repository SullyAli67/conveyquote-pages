Questions

Good to know.

What is conveyancing?

Conveyancing is the legal work involved in transferring ownership of a property, from checking the title and contract through to exchange, completion and registration.

What goes into a conveyancing quote?

The firm’s legal fee plus VAT, and disbursements paid to third parties on your behalf, such as searches, the Land Registry fee and, when buying, Stamp Duty Land Tax (Land Transaction Tax in Wales).

Why do quotes differ between firms?

Firms set their own legal fees and may charge differently for extra work such as leasehold matters. Itemised quotes make those differences easy to see.

Do I have to instruct a firm after getting a quote?

No. You can review your quote and decide in your own time.
